Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how INCAI ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the AI Health Android app on Google Play (the "app"). AI Health helps you explore health concerns through photo analysis and symptom questions with an AI assistant, keep health records and reminders, receive care guidance and manage family profiles. Because these features involve health-related information, we explain below what is used, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Health information you enter (photos, symptom questions, records, reminders, family profiles) is used to provide the app's features.
  • The app shows ads. Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, are used for advertising and to measure and improve the app.
  • We do not sell your health information and we do not use it to personalize ads.
  • AI responses are general information, not a medical diagnosis or treatment.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, limit ad personalization, and ask us to access or delete your information.

Information we handle

Information you choose to provide

To use the app's features, you may enter or upload:

  • Photos you take or select for photo analysis;
  • Questions and messages you send to the AI assistant, such as symptom descriptions;
  • Health records and reminders you create, and changes you track over time;
  • Family profile information you add for people you care for.

Providing this information is optional, but some features do not work without it. Only enter information about other people, including family members, if you have their permission or are authorized to act for them.

Device identifiers and technical information

The app collects device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, to serve ads and to optimize the app. Advertising and measurement services commonly also receive technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

How we use information

  • To provide the features: analyzing photos you submit, answering your questions with the AI assistant, keeping your records, reminders and family profiles, and giving care guidance and tips.
  • To show ads: using the Advertising ID and technical information to deliver ads, limit repeated ads, and measure ad performance.
  • To maintain and improve the app: understanding performance and fixing problems.
  • To communicate with you: responding to feedback and support requests you send by email.
  • To protect users and comply with law: preventing fraud and abuse and meeting legal obligations.

AI features and health information

Health information is sensitive, and we treat it with extra care:

  • Photos and questions you submit are processed by automated AI systems to generate a response. They are used to provide the feature you requested.
  • We do not sell health information, and we do not use it to personalize ads or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not use your health information for decisions about your eligibility for insurance, credit or employment.
  • Avoid including information you do not want processed, such as full names, ID numbers or images that identify other people.

AI Health provides general information and care guidance. AI responses can be incomplete or inaccurate and are not a medical diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Advertising and the Advertising ID

The app is supported by advertising. Ads may be personalized (based on the Advertising ID and information about your device and app activity) or non-personalized (based on general context, such as the app itself). Where applicable law requires consent for personalized advertising, we ask for it, and you can choose non-personalized ads.

The Advertising ID is a resettable identifier provided by Android. We use it only for advertising and analytics, and we do not link it to your health information. You can control it on your device:

  • Reset or delete the Advertising ID: open your device Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Limit personalized ads: on older Android versions, turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization in the same menu.

After you delete the Advertising ID or opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to run the app:

  • Service providers that process information on our behalf, such as AI processing and hosting, under obligations to protect it;
  • Advertising and measurement partners, which receive the Advertising ID and technical information described above, not your health information;
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and others;
  • Business transfers, if the app is transferred to another provider, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Device permissions

The app may ask for permissions such as the camera or photos (for photo analysis) and notifications (for reminders). Permissions are requested when you use the related feature, and you can change them at any time in your device settings. Denying a permission only affects the feature that needs it.

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. You can delete records, reminders and family profiles you created in the app, and you can ask us to delete information associated with you.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections, to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent you gave. To make a request, email us at ltinc9986@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. You can also stop all collection by the app by uninstalling it.

Children's privacy

AI Health is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian may manage a child's information through family profiles. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact INCAI at ltinc9986@gmail.com.
