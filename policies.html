Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains privacy information for Health Care, provided by INCAI.

Information and its purpose

Health Care does not directly collect personal data, apart from any data that third-party services, if present, process independently.

The app provides tools for exploring health concerns, organizing health records, tracking changes, setting reminders, and managing family profiles. For questions about how information entered into these tools is handled, please contact INCAI.

Third-party services

No third-party services are specified in the information supplied for this policy. Contact us if you need clarification about service providers or their handling of information.

Children

Health Care is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

Storage and security

Specific storage locations, retention periods, and security measures have not been supplied for this policy. Please contact INCAI for details before sharing sensitive information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy requests

You may contact INCAI to request access to or deletion of personal information, or to ask questions about information handling. Describe your request without including unnecessary health details. We will explain what information, if any, is available to us and how your request can be addressed.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this policy when you have questions about privacy.
