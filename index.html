FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI Health?

AI Health is an Android app on Google Play: a personal AI-powered health companion that helps you explore health concerns, keep your health information in one place and receive personalized care guidance.

What can I ask the AI assistant?

You can ask symptom-related questions, ask for help understanding your scan results, or request healthy lifestyle tips. The assistant gives helpful information to make your concerns easier to understand.

How does photo analysis work?

Take or choose a photo in the app to explore potential health concerns, then continue with your AI assistant to get helpful information and relevant guidance.

Can I keep records and reminders?

Yes. Keep personal health records organized — such as medications, allergies, conditions and vaccinations — track changes over time and set reminders that fit your routine.

Can I use it for my family?

Yes. Family profiles let you keep important health information for yourself and your loved ones organized in one place.
