What you can do

Understand. Organize. Care.

Whether you are looking after yourself or supporting loved ones, AI Health keeps your everyday health information accessible in one place.

Understand

Understand your health better

Explore potential health concerns with photo analysis, ask symptom-related questions, and interact with your AI assistant to get helpful information and find relevant guidance.

Organize

Your health, all in one place

Maintain personal health records, track changes over time, and set reminders that fit your daily routine — without managing everything separately.

Guidance

Personalized care guidance

Get AI-powered insights, daily tips, and progress support based on your needs, and build a more organized everyday health routine.

Family

Care for you and your family

Manage family profiles and keep important health information organized and accessible, whether you are looking after yourself or supporting loved ones.

How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Set up your profiles

    Install AI Health on your Android phone and add profiles for yourself and your family.

  2. Ask and explore

    Use photo analysis and symptom questions with your AI assistant to explore health concerns.

  3. Stay organized

    Keep records, track changes over time, set reminders and follow daily tips.
